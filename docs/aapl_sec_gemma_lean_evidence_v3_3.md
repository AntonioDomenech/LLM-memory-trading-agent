## The only source-format repair

V3.3 changes only the treatment of the company field in an official quarterly
master row. Parsing remains row-complete and proceeds in this fixed order:

1. authenticate the one capped gzip member, canonical header, and line
   controls;
2. for each physical data row, split
   `CIK|Company Name|Form Type|Date Filed|Filename` into exactly five fields,
   reject a duplicate exact raw line, and append that unchanged line to the
   private raw-row order before field validation;
3. require a nonempty numeric input CIK of at most ten digits and canonicalize
   it to ten digits, require a nonempty form, require a canonical filed date
   inside the requested quarter, and require a nonempty filename;
4. compute one Apple-target predicate from canonical CIK `0000320193` and form
   exactly `10-K` or `10-Q`;
5. require a nonempty company when that predicate is true, and otherwise allow
   the company field alone to be empty;
6. reuse the same predicate for the unchanged Apple path, accession,
   uniqueness, and target projection rules; and
7. only after the full loop succeeds, seal the raw-row count and physical-order
   hash plus the projected Apple targets.

One computed target predicate must be reused for both the company gate and
target projection. Independently duplicated predicates are forbidden. The
general rule must accept any number of distinct blank-company non-target rows
in any quarter, not merely one row or one response.

Forbidden control bytes remain forbidden in company, form, and filename even
when company is otherwise optional. Every exact Apple target still must use
the canonical `edgar/data/320193/<accession>.txt` path, contain one unique exact
accession, and exact-set reconcile on accession, subject CIK, form, filed date,
and filename. All gzip, header, field-count, duplicate, CIK, date, quarter,
path, accession, Submissions, complete-header, availability, and text checks
remain byte-for-byte or semantically unchanged.

The repair adds no trimming, whitespace normalization, placeholder company,
new global filename rule, or new diagnostic/control-flow code.

The repair must not use a branch, table, exception, or literal keyed to the
observed year, quarter, row ordinal, CIK, form, filed date, filename, accession,
or row hash. It must not `continue`, discard, synthesize, normalize, or reorder
the blank-company row. A successful role keeps the raw response only in the
private blob and represents every non-target row in compact evidence through
`raw_row_count` and `raw_row_order_sha256`; raw row text never enters compact or
public evidence. The source evidence must change if that represented raw row
changes. A non-Apple row with form `10-K` or `10-Q` remains non-target; an Apple
row with another form remains non-target; neither fact weakens the exact Apple
10-K/10-Q rule.

  checkpoint, or probe marker is reused;
