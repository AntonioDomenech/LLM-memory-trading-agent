one-shot preflight reservation, Yahoo request, Ollama request, Gemma generation,
market-value read, prediction, action, return or performance calculation,
confirmation or live-style data open, paid API, broker action, or real-money

contact, or per-request length.

and V3.18 cannot proceed to official preflight.
